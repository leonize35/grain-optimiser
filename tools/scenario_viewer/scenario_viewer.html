One receiving day, two yard layouts

Twelve trucks arrive between 07:00 and 07:38. Each is sampled, waits for its grading result, weighs in, tips into a hopper and weighs out. Grain then travels from the hopper pits up the shared elevator to a bin that accepts its commodity and grade. The only difference between the two runs is how many trucks fit in the hopper yard.
