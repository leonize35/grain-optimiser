# Bootstrap delivery plan — gated, not calendar promises

Owner develops and tests largely with AI assistance, while holding a full-time job and completing a dissertation. R400/month Claude subscription is confirmed by user; all other cash costs are unverified assumptions. Do not promise full working engine within 30 days.

## Gate 0: Rights and evidence
Review NWU/university IP, employment agreements, Senwes access/confidentiality and research-data permissions. Review third-party/library licences and distribution rights. Seek appropriate independent legal advice and quote for professional indemnity before advising on capital investments. Preserve private IP records. Outcome: documented commercialisation pathway, not simply a checkbox.

## Gate 1: Prototype audit and freeze
Import source/test suite, run tests, capture versions/outputs, classify functionality, assess dispatcher and reservations. Outcome: reproducible baseline and gap matrix.

## Gate 2: Configurable headless engine
Two materially different site configurations under identical engine code, whole-route reservation, feasible-first/FIFO policies, grain mass balance and commodity compatibility. Outcome: automated tests and repeatable scenario outputs. Confirm initial engine supports full customer-facing activities; don't hide missing loading/sampling functionality.

## Gate 3: Model assurance
Automated invariants; targeted real-world operational walkthroughs; fit and validate first supported site for specific KPIs. Outcome: clear limits on permissible recommendations.

## Gate 4: Backend and job infrastructure
FastAPI (or equivalent), durable scenario versioning, async workers (RQ/Celery/Arq selected on deployment basis), replication logging, retries/cancellation, reproducible run records. Outcome: scenario runs complete after browser disconnect.

## Gate 5: Manager experience
Simple configuration wizard, typed visual network, validation errors, goal-driven scenario builder, baseline vs experiments, uncertainty and constraints, reports. Outcome: independent manager usability walkthrough.

## Gate 6: Pilot and scale
Agree pre-season paid pilot or paid study with prospective first customer; validate value, do not assume a guaranteed Senwes order. Rollout to additional sites only after evidence of reliable reconfiguration. Price R36,000/year/site as an **unvalidated hypothesis**; consider per-site pre-season pilot and paid onboarding alternatives.

## Definition of done for each gate
Actual executed tests, reproducible evidence, documented assumptions, issue tracker, clear decision to proceed or stop. Product 'market-ready' additionally requires security review, support, data handling and liability agreements.
